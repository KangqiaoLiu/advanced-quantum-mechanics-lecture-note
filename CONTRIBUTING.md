# Contributing

Suggestions and corrections to the physics, derivations, numerical examples, accessibility, and interactive experiments are welcome.

**Original course author and maintainer:** Kangqiao LIU. Contributions are reviewed for scientific correctness and consistency with the graduate course.

## How to contribute

1. **Report a problem:** open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/KangqiaoLiu/advanced-quantum-mechanics-lecture-note/issues/new/choose), naming the relevant section or experiment and providing an explicit calculation or a reproducible discrepancy.
2. **Suggest a new idea:** open an enhancement issue explaining the physical question and how students would benefit.
3. **Submit a fix:** fork the repository, create a branch, edit the relevant file, and open a [Pull Request](https://github.com/KangqiaoLiu/advanced-quantum-mechanics-lecture-note/pulls) describing the change, its checks, and any figures affected.
4. **Review:** the maintainer evaluates mathematical validity, physical assumptions, reproducibility, and clarity. The PR history preserves contributor identity and discussion.

Contributions can also be discussed by email: [kqliu@xhu.edu.cn](mailto:kqliu@xhu.edu.cn).

## Scientific checks

- Explain the physical setup, basis, units, parameters, and approximations.
- Provide sufficient intermediate steps for independent graduate-level study.
- Check normalization, an analytically accessible limit, or numerical convergence.
- Keep cross-chapter notation coherent and labels in figures in English.
- Add or update the relevant tests and ensure the existing GitHub Actions checks pass.
- Cite external sources accurately and confirm you are authorized to use contributed material.
