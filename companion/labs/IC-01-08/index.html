How does one keep track of a two-qubit state?

Construct a tensor-product state, apply a controlled gate, compute four joint amplitudes and perform a partial trace. Track the index ordering explicitly at every stage.

Motivation

Why do basis indices matter in a composite Hilbert space?

Two two-level quantum systems combine into a four-dimensional Hilbert space. Each component is indexed by a pair (i,j) referring to subsystem A and subsystem B, respectively. Matrix operations and partial traces require one consistent ordering. An initially separable tensor product has factorized coefficients, while a controlled-NOT can entangle the two qubits. Following both the four amplitudes and the resulting subsystem state reveals exactly where the correlations appear. The same convention extends to any many-particle or qubit-register calculation.

Predict first. Prepare A in |+x〉 and B in |0〉. Before and after a controlled-NOT with A as the control, determine the four nonzero amplitudes, the local state of A, and the entropy of its reduced density operator.

Prerequisites: Tensor products, normalized two-component kets, projectors, unitary gates and the definition of a partial trace.

Bipartite state geometry

How much entanglement can CNOT create?

Entanglement is quantified by the reduced von Neumann entropy of qubit A because the full joint state remains pure.

For each α, the two-qubit state is constructed and ρA is traced out. The entropy reaches one bit when A is balanced and B is a computational-basis state; it stays zero for an initially separable identity operation.

Derive the result

Index the coefficients before tracing out a subsystem

Prepare |a〉=cos(α/2)|0〉+sin(α/2)|1〉 and |b〉=cos(β/2)|0〉+sin(β/2)|1〉. In the fixed basis {|00〉,|01〉,|10〉,|11〉}, the tensor product has coefficient vector (a₀b₀,a₀b₁,a₁b₀,a₁b₁)ᵀ. A controlled-NOT with A as control and B as target swaps the last two entries.

|a〉⊗|b〉=Σᵢⱼ aᵢ bⱼ|ij〉;   CNOT|i,j〉=|i,j⊕i〉.

For a general pure joint state |ψ〉=Σijcij|ij〉, the full density matrix is ρAB=|ψ〉〈ψ|. Tracing out B leaves the 2×2 matrix (ρA)ik=Σj cij c*kj. In particular, the reduced diagonal probabilities obey (ρA)00=|c00|²+|c01|² and (ρA)11=|c10|²+|c11|².

(ρA)ik = Σⱼ cᵢⱼ c*ₖⱼ,   TrρA=1.

Its eigenvalues λ± determine the local entropy −Σλlog₂λ. Because the full state is pure, this entropy is exactly the pure-state bipartite entanglement entropy. Replacing cij by a single flattened index without remembering the pair assignment would corrupt the partial trace and potentially change the predicted physical correlations.

Exactly soluble limits and sanity checks

If α=0, A is |0〉 and the controlled gate acts trivially: all states remain separable. If β=90°, B is |+x〉, a +1 eigenstate of X; the target flip changes nothing and CNOT leaves the tensor product separable. For α=90°, β=0 and CNOT, the state becomes (|00〉+|11〉)/√2 with ρA=I/2 and one bit of entanglement entropy.

What assumptions define this model?

Two ideal qubits, pure real y-rotation preparations and a perfect computational-basis controlled-NOT with fixed control/target assignment. Complex phases and noisy gates are further extensions; the index and partial trace formulas remain valid in all cases.

Self-directed physics exercises

Three independent investigations

  1. Construct the Bell pair. Set α=90°, β=0 and compare the identity and controlled-NOT operations. Verify that only |00〉 and |11〉 remain with equal probabilities after CNOT.
  2. Trace out B explicitly. Calculate ρA using the two values of the B index in each matrix element. Verify that local purity drops to one half in the Bell state while the global state remains pure.
  3. Find a zero-entanglement control. Set B to |+x〉 and vary A. Observe that CNOT leaves a product state because the target is an eigenvector of X. Explain why an entangling gate does not entangle every input.

Self-check: In the ordering |00〉,|01〉,|10〉,|11〉, which coefficients does CNOT with A as control exchange?

Extension: replace the real rotations by complex spinors and verify that the partial trace uses complex conjugation in cij c*kj.

Scientific meaning

Conclusions, caveats and further reading

The tensor product is conceptually straightforward once every Hilbert-space factor and flattened index is specified. The laboratory makes normalization, entangling operations, conditional subsystem probabilities and partial traces directly inspectable. These operations recur in measurement instruments, quantum information and many-body Fock constructions.
