Motivation and learning objective

Why can the net transport over a full cycle remain quantized while the instantaneous curvature changes continuously?

A Rice–Mele-type alternating lattice can transport an integer number of particles per adiabatic cycle when its control loop winds around an isolated gap-closing point. Compute the full momentum–cycle Berry curvature and its integrated pump index while moving the control loop into and out of the topological regime.

Predict before exploring. With a closed loop centered at (δ,Δ)=(0,0), infer whether the gap stays open for nonzero radius. Shift the loop center outside its radius and predict the net topological pump index.

Recommended prerequisites: the relevant mathematical formulation in the English lecture notes, complex amplitudes, expectation values, and a basic understanding of quantum operators. Read the derivation below before interpreting the plots as physical observables.

Derivation and assumptions

Reconstruct the physical calculation

Use the Rice–Mele family H(k,φ)=d(k,φ)·σ with d=(t₁+t₂cosk,t₂sink,Δ). Set t₁=t₀+δ, t₂=t₀−δ, t₀=1, δ(φ)=δ₀+Rcosφ and Δ(φ)=Δ₀+Rsinφ. The control point traces a circle in the δ–Δ plane, encircling the critical point (0,0) whenever √(δ₀²+Δ₀²)<R.

d=(t₁+t₂cosk,t₂sink,Δ);   Ω₋(k,φ)=[d·(∂kd×∂φd)]/[2|d|³].

For the occupied lower band and Berry convention A=i〈u|∂u〉, the two-dimensional integral over momentum and cyclic parameter is C=(1/2π)∫₀²πdφ∫BZdk Ω₋(k,φ). This is a Chern number on the momentum–time torus whenever the occupied band stays separated from the empty band over the entire cycle.

The discrete numerical model computes d and both derivatives analytically and integrates their triple product by a periodic midpoint quadrature. This avoids finite-difference gauge discontinuities. A control loop enclosing the gap-closing point yields C=−1 for the stated Fourier, pump orientation and lower-band conventions; a displaced loop that excludes the origin yields C=0. Reversing pump direction reverses the index.

Define the cumulative Berry-flux charge index Q(φ)=(1/2π)∫₀φdφ′∫BZdk Ω₋(k,φ′). It changes smoothly during the cycle and becomes quantized at φ=2π. Its physical sign relative to an electrical charge current depends on carrier charge and transport orientation, so the display reports the convention-fixed geometric index, not an unqualified signed electron current.

Check exact and singular limits

For δ₀=Δ₀=0 and R>0, the loop winds once and the lower-band index approaches −1. When the circle lies entirely outside the origin, it approaches zero. If R equals √(δ₀²+Δ₀²), the path crosses a bulk degeneracy at k=π and the isolated-band Chern number is undefined.

Numerical and physical validity conditions

Noninteracting two-band Rice–Mele lattice at half filling, infinite periodic bulk, isolated occupied lower band, slow adiabatic motion, fixed baseline hopping t₀=1, and a closed pump cycle. Finite-duration corrections, boundary charge redistribution and charge-specific current signs are beyond the model.

Active understanding

Three independent investigations

  1. Show quantization. Choose a centered loop, change momentum mesh N, and verify that the final integrated index approaches −1.
  2. Leave the topological region. Shift Δ₀ beyond the radius. Examine the loop and determine why the indexed winding falls to zero without requiring changes in the instantaneous signal formula.
  3. Approach a singular cycle. Bring the loop close to the degeneracy. Inspect the minimum spectral gap, Berry peaks and slower numerical convergence; explain why the exactly critical cycle has no isolated-band invariant.

Self-check: When does an isolated occupied band have a well-defined quantized Thouless pump index for this loop?

Extension: reproduce one model with a separate implementation or an AI-assisted notebook. Check numerical convergence, normalizations, phase conventions, or independent limiting-case results before trusting the answer.

Physical conclusions

Interpret the invariant or approximation carefully

A Thouless pump is a dynamical manifestation of band topology: smoothly varying instantaneous responses integrate to a robust integer across a closed, gapped adiabatic cycle. Numerical integration, loop geometry and the spectral gap provide independent checks of the result.
