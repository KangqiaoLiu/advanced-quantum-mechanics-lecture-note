Experiment 03 · Bulk topology and boundaries

Where do the SSH chain's edge states come from?

Change the two hopping amplitudes, observe the Bloch bands, trace the complex winding loop, and diagonalize a finite open chain. Test what happens when the gap closes, the ends connect, or chiral symmetry is broken.

15–35 min core experimentExact finite-chain diagonalizationWinding and bulk gapsBoundary-state localization

Start from a question

Can a property of bulk Bloch states predict boundary behavior?

The Su–Schrieffer–Heeger chain alternates an intracell coupling t₁ and an intercell coupling t₂. It has only two sublattice orbitals, A and B, per unit cell. Nevertheless, its infinite periodic system can carry a nontrivial winding number, and a finite open chain with the corresponding termination develops states concentrated near its ends. Both statements require care about gap, symmetry, unit-cell choice and boundary termination.

Predict first: for positive t₁ and t₂, what happens as t₂ crosses t₁? Watch the bulk gap close and reopen. Does the open finite chain acquire states at precisely zero energy, or can finite-size hybridization split them?

From hopping to topology

Deriving the bulk winding

With the note's Fourier convention an=N−1/2∑keiknak, the Bloch Hamiltonian is

h(k) = [[Δ, q(k)], [q*(k), −Δ]],   q(k)=t₁+t₂e−ik.

For Δ=0, Γ=σz anticommutes with h(k), and the Bloch vector lies in a plane. Its loop, seen as q*(k)=t₁+t₂eik, winds around zero whenever the circle radius t₂ exceeds its center displacement t₁.

E±(k)=±√[t₁²+t₂²+2t₁t₂ cos k+Δ²],   gap=2√[(t₁−t₂)²+Δ²].
Why does the winding become undefined at t₁=t₂?

At k=π, q(k)=t₁−t₂. Equality makes the complex loop pass through its reference origin. Its phase is then undefined at that momentum. At Δ=0 the bulk gap closes at the same point. When Δ≠0, the energy gap can remain open, but chiral symmetry and the resulting protected winding classification have already been lost.

Where do the boundary amplitudes come from?

For a semi-infinite chiral chain, the E=0 sublattice equations imply t₁ Aj+t₂ Aj+1=0, hence Aj+1=−(t₁/t₂)Aj. A normalizable left-localized mode requires |t₁/t₂|<1 for this termination. On a finite chain, left and right edge wavefunctions overlap and generally create two eigenenergies ±ε instead of exact zero. Compare the splitting as N increases.

What does the Zak phase measure?

The lower Bloch band's Berry connection is A(k)=i〈u₋|∂ku₋〉. With the specified Bloch basis and unit cell, γZak=πν modulo 2π when the band remains gapped and chiral symmetry protects the quantization. An orbital-position or unit-cell convention change requires tracking its effect on Berry phases; observe the present q*(k) loop only under the convention stated above.

Controlled investigations

Experiments that reveal the assumptions

  1. Topological transition. Set t₂=1, sweep t₁ through 1. Check the gap and whether ν changes. The exact winding value is undefined at the transition.
  2. Boundary versus bulk. In the t₂>t₁ regime, compare N=4 to N=16. Select the state nearest E=0 and observe localization and finite-size energy splitting. Then change to periodic boundaries: the physical ends disappear.
  3. Break chiral symmetry. Turn on Δ. The periodic band gap can stay open while the protected winding label disappears. Compare the finite-chain energies and the q loop, now identified as a reference curve only.

Self-check: for Δ=0, t₁=0.6 and t₂=1.3, what is the bulk winding ν in the chapter's convention?

Further study: distinguish the chiral 1D SSH classification from the 2D Berry-curvature/Chern-number calculation later in the chapter.

Scientific scope and manuscript links

The finite Hamiltonian uses a real symmetric 2N×2N matrix, exactly diagonalized for the displayed parameter choices. The model describes a single-particle tight-binding band; filling and many-body interactions are outside this experiment. The selected termination is explicit. A nonzero staggered Δ is an illustrative symmetry-breaking deformation and the two-band q loop is not a protected topological invariant in that case. At t₁=t₂, Δ=0, the undefined winding is shown explicitly.
