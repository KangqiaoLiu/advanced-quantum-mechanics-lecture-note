# Interactive Lab Template

**Lab ID:** `IC-XX-YY`  
**Chapter and source:** `chapters/chXX/actual_source.tex` and semantic equation labels (when present)  
**Status:** planned → implemented only after independent validation  
**Depth:** foundation / guided / advanced  
**Learning paths:** classroom-core / extension; prerequisites and next topics

## 1. Physical question
One answerable physical question. What would the student predict before interacting? What are the physical assumptions?

## 2. Model and conventions
Hilbert space and basis; Hamiltonian/state/observable; units; boundary conditions; mathematical formulas and exact correspondence to the current lecture notes. Record analytically solvable benchmarks and conditions of validity.

## 3. Interactive views and controls
Default state, parameter ranges and units, 1–3 primary controls, observable readouts and axes, optional advanced controls. Explain the meaning of each output. Show exact, approximated and sampled results distinctly.

## 4. Guided route: predict → observe → explain
- Prediction prompt with meaningful answer.
- Three or fewer deliberate parameter changes, with a clear comparison.
- Progressive algebraic explanation linked to the notes and equations.
- Short self-check with feedback about assumptions and interpretation.

## 5. Explore and connect
A nontrivial limiting case, altered assumption, boundary or approximation regime. Connection to one other chapter and an optional independent calculation.

## 6. Numerical and analytic verification
Record fixtures and expected tolerance. Check normalization/probability conservation or trace; Hermiticity/unitarity/physical symmetry where relevant; known limiting results; signs, phase conventions, finite-size effects and parameter edge conditions. An independent reference computation is required for models with numerical diagonalization or evolution.

## 7. Accessibility and editorial review
English-only labels and prompts; keyboard operation; visible numerical values; responsive layout; reduced-motion/static explanation; actual axis units; no overlapping annotations or truncated equations; literature/source and image rights checked.

## 8. Status and provenance
Model implementation, tests, screenshot/inspection note, lab content revision and release-ready state; update the master `catalog.json`. A navigable catalog entry is not itself a completed lab.
