\small
\noindent Throughout these notes, \emph{hats} denote abstract operators, \emph{bold symbols}
denote spatial vectors, and a dagger denotes the Hermitian adjoint.
Matrices in a specified basis may omit hats. The superscript $^{\mathsf T}$ denotes
ordinary matrix transpose; $\dagger$ includes complex conjugation. The symbol
$\mathbf1$ always denotes the identity (with a subsystem subscript when needed).
Subscripts identify indices, states, or representations as indicated;
local parameters are defined where introduced.
